# PRODUCT.md — Little Blue World

## Product
**Little Blue World — for Alia** is a portrait Android birthday APK that feels like exploring a tiny handcrafted pixel-ocean diorama made specifically for Alia.

Recipient: **Pradipa Fauziyyah Alya**  
Display name: **Alia**  
Birthday: **23 July**

The app never labels the relationship between author and recipient.

## Experience goals
The product should feel:
- playful first,
- personal and warm underneath,
- subtly flirty in small moments,
- sincere at the Wish/finale,
- self-aware and funny at the author reveal.

Tone ratio:
- 50% playful
- 30% sweet
- 20% subtle flirting

Avoid generic birthday-template energy, excessive romance, Valentine styling, childish UI, and obvious AI-template composition.

## Core experience
1. Pixel Boot / staged loading
2. Birthday Entrance
3. Little Blue World
4. Alia Archive
5. Message Bottles
6. Collectible Stars
7. Tiny Café
8. Shell surprise
9. Fish Easter Egg
10. Make a Wish
11. Final birthday message
12. About the Author
13. Ending
14. Post-finale transformed World / revisit

## Main progression
Main discoveries:
- Alia Archive
- Message Bottles
- Collectible Stars
- Tiny Café

Wish unlock threshold: **3 of 4**.

Shell, Fish, and About the Author are optional discoveries.

## Product principles
- Environment first, interface second.
- Discovery should feel natural; no generic home/menu dashboard.
- Rich experience, lightweight implementation.
- Offline failure must never break the birthday experience.
- The Wish backend exists only for one explicit user-consented submission.
- No analytics, account system, tracking, admin panel, cloud photo storage, or remote CMS.

## Post-finale
After the ending:
- World uses evening/dusk state
- moon appears
- slightly richer star field
- one extra flower appears
- Fish dialogue changes
- Wish is considered already made
- normal revisits skip the Birthday Entrance

## Audio
Audio is optional until real audio assets are supplied. The application must remain complete and polished when silent.
